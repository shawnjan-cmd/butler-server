​Our privacy philosophy is simple: Your data is your own. The App is built on a local-first, privacy-centric architecture. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any of your personal information.
​1. Information We Do NOT Collect
​We do not operate remote servers to collect your data. Specifically, we do not collect, store, or transmit:

​2. Device Permissions and Usage
​To provide its core functionality, the App requires certain on-device permissions. All data processed via these permissions remains strictly local to your device.
​Camera Permission: The App requests camera access solely for the purpose of scanning QR codes to pair your mobile device with your local desktop server. The camera feed is processed locally and discarded immediately. No images or videos are captured, stored, or transmitted to us or any third party.
​Local Network (LAN) Access: The App communicates directly over your Local Area Network to interface with your own PC (to fetch system vitals, execute scripts, and send commands). This traffic is routed directly between your mobile device and your computer. It never passes through our servers.
​Local Storage / File System: The App uses your device's secure local storage to save user preferences, custom HTML components, scripts, and pairing configurations (such as your local IP and port). This data never leaves your device and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the App or clear its cache.
​3. Third-Party AI Services and API Keys
​The App includes features (such as "Butler AI") that allow you to interact with third-party Large Language Models (e.g., Anthropic Claude, OpenAI).
​API Key Storage: You must provide your own API keys to use these features. These keys are stored locally on your device in an encrypted format and are never sent to us.
​Direct Communication: Your prompts, messages, and API keys are transmitted directly from your device to the respective third-party provider's API endpoints.
​Third-Party Policies: We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We strongly encourage you to review the privacy policies of any AI provider whose API keys you use within the App.
​4. Data Security
​Because we do not collect your data, the security of your information relies entirely on the security of your own device and local network. We use standard React Native / Expo secure storage mechanisms (AsyncStorage and expo-file-system) to keep your configurations safe on your phone.
​5. Children's Privacy
​Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.
